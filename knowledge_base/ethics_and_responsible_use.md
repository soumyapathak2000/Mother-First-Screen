# Ethics and responsible use

## Data

- Only **synthetic** data is used in this repository. No names, phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers or real health records are included.
- In any future real data collection, data would be anonymised or coded, collected only after informed consent, and stored securely. Dashboards show aggregate counts only.

## Screening

- The Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS) is a **screening** tool. A high score is a reason to offer assessment, not a diagnosis.
- The EPDS items are not reproduced in this repository. A licensed, validated local-language version should be used, with the authors and source cited.
- Any answer to the self-harm item needs an immediate human response and referral.

## Use of AI

- AI tools are **not** used to score, diagnose or decide referrals.
- Generative AI is used only to draft awareness scripts and plain-language summaries.
- A native speaker or health professional reviews all AI-drafted or translated content before use.
- Every AI-generated statement about the dashboard is checked against the data before it is used.
- The tools and prompts used are recorded.

## Approvals

- Ethics clearance from an institutional ethics committee and permission from the health department are required before any field work or real data collection.

## Limitations

- This is a student design project. It has not been clinically validated or tested with real participants.
- The placeholder cut-off of 10 in the synthetic data is not a recommendation.
- Gaps described in the synopsis (for example about existing apps and services) are working premises that still need to be validated with health staff.
