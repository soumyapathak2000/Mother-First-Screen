# Screening and referral protocol (design draft)

> **Draft for design and discussion only.** It has not been reviewed by a health authority or ethics committee. A local health department protocol always takes priority.

## Principles

- **Screening is not diagnosis.** A score tells us who should be offered further assessment, not what condition a woman has.
- **No AI in the screening step.** The score is the sum of the ten item scores, compared with a cut-off.
- **Every positive result has a next step.** Nobody is screened without a referral path.
- **Consent and privacy come first.** The woman agrees before she is screened, and results are shared only with those who need them to help her.

## Flow

1. **Greeting and consent.** The ASHA or ANM worker explains the purpose in simple language and asks for consent. The woman may stop at any time.
2. **Administer the EPDS** in the woman's language. If she cannot read, use the audio instructions or read the items aloud in a private place, without suggesting answers.
3. **Automatic scoring** in the form: total score (0 to 30) and the score on the self-harm item.
4. **Decide the next step:**
   - **Below cut-off and self-harm item is 0:** thank her, share the awareness message and tell her where to get help if she feels worse later.
   - **At or above cut-off:** explain gently that the result suggests she may benefit from talking to a health professional, and refer her to the PHC medical officer. Share Tele-MANAS **14416**.
   - **Any answer above 0 on the self-harm item:** treat as **urgent**. Do not leave her alone, tell the PHC medical officer straight away, share Tele-MANAS 14416, and call the emergency number **112** if she is in immediate danger.
5. **Record the referral status** (Completed, Pending, Declined, Tele-MANAS contacted) so that supervisors can follow up.
6. **Follow up** on pending referrals according to the local plan.

## Who does what

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| ASHA / ANM | Consent, administer the EPDS, give the referral, record status |
| PHC medical officer | Assess referred women and decide on care |
| Supervisor | Review anonymised counts and follow up on pending referrals |
| Mental health professional (guide) | Review the protocol, training and translations |
