Summaries below are based on abstracts and summaries of the sources. Check figures against the original papers before quoting them.

| 1 | Upadhyay RP et al. (2017). Postpartum depression in India: a systematic review and meta-analysis. *Bulletin of the WHO* 95(10):706-717 | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5689195/ | Pooled prevalence of postpartum depression in India about 22% (95% CI 19-25).
